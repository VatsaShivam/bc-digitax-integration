Privacy

How the DigiTax UAE Invoice Connector and this storefront handle data.

Data sent to DigiTax

When a user sends a posted sales invoice, the connector sends the invoice number and dates, mapped buyer and item IDs, quantities, prices, discounts, VAT, payment details, included notes and references, and a Business Central record identifier to DigiTax over HTTPS. Payment details can include a payee bank account or direct-debit mandate identifier when the payment method requires them.

The connector also calls DigiTax to verify the selected business, browse invoices, and retrieve submission status. It authenticates those requests with the company API key.

Data stored in Business Central

The API key is stored in encrypted, company-scoped isolated storage. The connector stores configuration, mappings, and submission records in the company. Submission records include DigiTax identifiers, timestamps, and status or error details. Rows loaded for browsing DigiTax invoices are temporary.

Access and administration

Business Central permissions control who can configure the connector, submit invoices, and browse DigiTax invoice records. In DigiTax UAE Setup, an administrator can replace or remove the API key. Removing the key stops authenticated connector requests and leaves previously submitted invoices in DigiTax.

Storefront and questions

This storefront uses no forms or analytics scripts. Search runs in your browser. The website host may process request information to deliver the pages and downloads.

For connector data questions, contact your Business Central administrator or the provider who installed it. For information about records held by DigiTax, use the Support option in the DigiTax dashboard.
